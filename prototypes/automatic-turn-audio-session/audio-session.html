Operator Session audio-policy prototype

Question: can one Operator Session support dependable push-to-talk and optional automatic half duplex now, while leaving independent capture and playback activities available for a later full-duplex experiment?

Prototype rule: a hard interruption preserves completed audible sentences and accepted Action Records. It cancels the active Response Context, discards the unfinished audio and any unaccepted proposal, and records only an interruption event—never the hidden remainder of generated speech.
