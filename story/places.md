# Places

The twelve Subscriber Lines connect physical places, not factions or individual NPCs. Different people can use the telephone at each place. The operator recognizes the place but must determine which person, faction, or lie is speaking from it.

1. **Neeladesh Central Hospital:** The president in the ICU; the operator's pregnant wife; doctors; nurses; presidential guards; ruling-party visitors; patients and their relatives.
2. **Republic Secretariat:** Cabinet ministers; ruling-party officials; ministry clerks; messengers; cleaners; secret-police informants; opposition sympathizers hiding among the staff.
3. **Home Affairs Annex:** Police commanders; State Protection Directorate officers; records clerks; informants; lawyers; detainees; relatives trying to locate someone who has disappeared.
4. **Nabinagar Cantonment:** Army generals; junior officers; conscripts; military doctors; radio operators; civilian cooks and drivers; prisoners held during the emergency.
5. **National Radio Building:** Newsreaders; producers; technicians; ruling-party censors; opposition sympathizers; politicians arriving to make announcements; armed groups trying to seize the transmitter.
6. **South Neeladesh Embassy:** Diplomats; intelligence officers; local employees; translators; asylum seekers; northern politicians making secret visits; protesters gathered outside.
7. **Grand Neela Hotel:** Foreign journalists; mine owners; South Neeladesh agents; visiting politicians; opposition leaders; wealthy guests; hotel workers who overhear more than anyone realizes.
8. **Ratan Mining Company Office:** Company directors; engineers; accountants; government inspectors; worker representatives; private guards; officials negotiating coal shipments.
9. **Ratan Colony:** Miners; miners' families; union organizers; shopkeepers; government informants; True North Brigade recruiters; local doctors and teachers.
10. **Shapla Apartments:** Minority families; government clerks; an army officer's family; autonomy organizers; informants; students; ordinary tenants trying to avoid politics.
11. **Kheyaghat Market:** Merchants; porters; smugglers; couriers; nationalist organizers; minority traders; shoppers; children carrying messages for money.
12. **Nabinagar Central Station:** Dispatchers; freight clerks; railway guards; soldiers; refugees; mine workers; political couriers; passengers trapped by cancelled trains.
