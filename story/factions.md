# Factions

## National Reconstruction Party

The president's ruling party wants to survive without admitting that its leader can no longer rule. It controls ministries, state radio, senior police officials, and access to the ICU. It pressures doctors, blocks visitors, and issues orders in the president's name.

## State Protection Directorate

The State Protection Directorate is the secret police, separate from the army. It monitors every faction, recruits informants, arrests suspected conspirators, and wants the next government to depend on it. It protects the ruling party for now but competes with the army for control of the emergency.

## National Army Command

The army wants an emergency military government until the country is "safe." It can occupy the capital, close transport routes, seize communications offices, and prevent civilian leaders from meeting. Some officers want order. Others believe winning the war entitled the army to rule.

## Red Earth Workers' Congress

The mine-based opposition can halt coal production and railway shipments. It wants the ruling party removed, political prisoners released, labor protections, and elections. Its leaders must decide whether they still represent workers once national power is within reach.

## Riverland Autonomy League

The minority political movement wants regional autonomy, local government, minority-language schools, local policing, and protection from nationalist mobs. Some members collect evidence and organize evacuations. Others have armed themselves after the police failed to protect their communities.

## True North Brigade

The True North Brigade is a domestic terrorist movement aligned with the president and his nationalist politics. It attacks minorities and opposition organizers while claiming to defend North Neeladesh. It uses bombings, threats, and manufactured incidents to provoke retaliation. Nobody knows whether it had any role in the attempt on the president's life.

## Government of South Neeladesh

South Neeladesh wants a friendly puppet government in the North. It offers medicine, fuel, intelligence, money, and diplomatic recognition while secretly contacting northern politicians and officers.

## People's Transition Council

The People's Transition Council is a respectable civilian opposition made up of lawyers, professors, business owners, and former officials. It promises democratic government, but South Neeladesh funds and influences its leadership.

## Mine Owners' Compact

The mine-owning families and companies want production and exports to continue. They will support the ruling party, army, or civilian opposition if that side can break strikes, keep taxes low, and protect their property.

Senior judges may appear as individual NPCs, but they are not a faction.
