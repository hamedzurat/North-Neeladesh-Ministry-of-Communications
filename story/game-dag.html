NORTH NEELADESH / STORY DESIGN REVIEW

EVERY CALL OPENS A DIFFERENT ROAD

This is a true call-node DAG. A node is one call. Each outcome is an edge to another call or an ending. The LLM supplies natural language for the named caller; the DAG decides what the caller knows, what the operator can report, and what happens next.

Player state: cash, food, medicine, wife safety, evidence, faction trust, arrests, evacuation progress, radio control, and army control. A Tap Bridge only adds evidence. Evidence changes the DAG only when the operator later says it to someone and that person acts on it. One call has one audible speaker.

World settings

  • North Neeladesh was created three years ago when an ethnic war split Neeladesh. The border and graves are both recent.
  • The president has ruled since partition. His government is responsible for killings and mob violence against minorities.
  • An unknown attacker shot the president. He is alive in intensive care but cannot rule, confirm an order, or contradict anyone speaking for him.
  • The country is a bedside state: the ruling party controls access to the ICU while the army, secret police, opposition, nationalists, autonomy movement, mine owners, and South Neeladesh compete for succession.
  • The operator's father became popular during the war and was killed by sympathizers after it ended. The ministry gave the operator a job to support the bereaved family.
  • A neighboring member of the persecuted minority was killed by a mob. The operator remembers the neighbor by name, but is not himself a member of that minority.
  • The operator is twenty-four, works at the Ministry of Communications, and has a pregnant wife at the hospital.
  • He dislikes the nepotism that put him behind the switchboard, but cannot afford to refuse the job.
  • The operator is not a faction member. At first the factions do not know his name and treat him as infrastructure. His power comes from deciding who can speak to whom.
  • The switchboard has twelve subscriber lines, one Tap Bridge, emergency Police/EMS calls, and a Directory whose records describe what the state believes, not necessarily what is true.
  • Side characters and their relationships with the operator matter even when they are not deciding national power.

Time and tone

  • The game covers four work shifts. Each shift contains four authored call nodes, with side calls mixed into the route.
  • Every call has one audible named speaker. Other people's words are reported or quoted by that speaker.
  • The LLM supplies natural wording and personality. The DAG supplies facts, permitted knowledge, destinations, actions, and outcomes.
  • The story is not a mystery about the operator's father. It is a pressure story about information, survival, and institutions deciding who may speak.
  • Money is practical: food, medicine, transport, and hospital access. It is not a morality score.

Possible game edges

EdgeOperator actionWhat it can do
Connect requested placeLook up the destination, ring it, crank, and connect.Lets the caller exchange information and usually advances to the next call.
Deliberate misrouteConnect the caller to a different Directory place.Arrest, misinformation, a faction gain, or an early ending.
Directory verificationInspect the record before deciding.Confirms identity, exposes a lie, or unlocks a safer edge.
DelayKeep the caller on the operator line or put the call on hold.Creates time for another call, but may cause the caller to expire.
Refuse or let expireDo not complete the route.Missed-call consequences, lost evidence, lost money, or family danger.
Tap and rememberConnect both ends through a Tap Bridge and listen.Adds a fact to operator knowledge. It does not branch until the operator later discloses it.
Disclose evidenceTell a later caller what was heard.That caller may arrest, evacuate, protect, broadcast, retaliate, or ignore.
Emergency serviceUse Police or EMS while handling a caller.Interrupts routing and saves or exposes people. A wrong service can waste the response.
Paid favorAccept money for priority, access, a warning, or a private message.Raises survival resources while changing trust and political exposure.
Protect familySpend time, money, or a connection on the wife and child.Preserves family safety but can close a political opportunity.

Detailed call cards

Each card describes the one-speaker voice turn. Tapping is only possible after the operator has connected the caller and destination through a Tap Bridge. The quoted tap result is what the player hears; it is not automatically shared with anyone.

C1 / Mina Das

The visitor list

Call: From Neeladesh Central Hospital, requests Republic Secretariat. Mina says the hospital is sending visitor names to the Secretariat. She quietly identifies the operator's pregnant wife as a mistaken entry and asks for a correction.

If tapped: "I copied the list before they sealed it. Your wife's name is beside the president's visitors. I do not know who added it."

Operator: Directory lookup verifies Mina, connect her, or delay/refuse. Verification -> C2; unchecked connection -> C3; refusal -> E1.

C2 / Elias Korr

The missing detainee

Call: From Home Affairs Annex, requests Neeladesh Central Hospital. Elias asks the hospital to confirm that Nabin Das's sister survived a police raid. He says Nabin was taken without a warrant.

If tapped: "Do not say Nabin's name to Home Affairs. The clerk there told me the file was moved to the basement after midnight."

Operator: Connect hospital -> C4; misroute back to Home Affairs -> E2; accept Elias's paid legal message -> C4 with cash +1.

C3 / Paro Sen / side

The paid warning

Call: From Kheyaghat Market, requests Ratan Colony. Paro offers supper money to warn a storekeeper that police are checking coal shipments. He will not initially name the faction paying him.

If tapped: "Tell the storekeeper to move the red sacks before sundown. The workers' people will pay you, not the police."

Operator: Connect -> C4 and cash +1; report to police -> C4 with police pressure; let expire -> C4 with no cash.

C4 / Anika Roy

Medicine through the hotel

Call: From Neeladesh Central Hospital, requests Grand Neela Hotel. Anika says a foreign doctor hid medicine in the hotel pharmacy and asks the operator to connect her without mentioning the operator's wife.

If tapped: "The medicine is for the maternity ward, but the embassy wants the president's doctors to receive it first. Decide before the night inventory."

Operator: Connect hotel -> C5 with medicine and wife safety; connect Secretariat -> E3; refuse -> C5, or E4 if cash is already below the food threshold.

C5 / Javed Rahman

The altered ledger

Call: From Ratan Mining Company Office, requests Nabinagar Central Station. Javed says the company changed coal shipment numbers after the strike began and asks the dispatcher to hold a train.

If tapped: "The original number is 412 wagons. The report says 289. The missing 123 are being loaded onto army trucks."

Operator: Connect station -> C6; connect Ratan Colony -> C7 with an unverified claim; report Javed to Home Affairs -> E5.

C6 / Mira Halek / tap

The military truck

Call: From Nabinagar Central Station, requests Ratan Mining Company Office. Mira wants the mine office to stop a shipment labelled as worker freight.

If tapped: "I can see the truck manifest. Captain Varo signed it. It is not coal and it is not going to the civilian depot. It leaves at 18:20."

Operator: Connect and tap -> C7 with evidence; connect without tapping -> C7 without evidence; let the call expire -> E6.

C7 / Tomas Vale / side

The journalist's bargain

Call: From Grand Neela Hotel, requests South Neeladesh Embassy. Tomas offers money for the room number of a People's Transition Council lawyer. He wants proof of southern influence.

If tapped: "The lawyer is not selling the country. She has three judges in that room and believes the embassy is the only place they will not be arrested."

Operator: Sell the room -> C8 with cash +2 and council trust -1; protect it -> C8 with trust +1; report Tomas -> E7.

C8 / Dilan Bose

The transmitter choice

Call: From National Radio Building, requests Nabinagar Cantonment. Dilan says a censor locked the transmitter room and asks the army for guards before a public announcement.

If tapped: "The draft announcement blames Riverland organizers for the president's shooting. The evidence is a blank page with a stamp on it."

Operator: Connect army or Secretariat -> C9 with different control flags; connect Home Affairs -> E8; tap and remember the accusation -> C9 with radio evidence.

C9 / Laleh Mir

The evacuation route

Call: From Shapla Apartments, requests Ratan Colony. Laleh asks for workers, drivers, and fuel to move minority families before the radio accusation spreads.

If tapped: "Do not write the children's names. If the police get a list, they will call it an evacuation register and use it as a target list."

Operator: Connect workers and report radio evidence -> C10; army escort -> C10 with names exposed; Home Affairs -> E9; expire -> C10 with evacuation late.

C10 / Suri Nahal

The evidence bargain

Call: From Republic Secretariat, requests South Neeladesh Embassy. Suri asks what the operator knows about arrests, the radio draft, and the military shipment.

If tapped: "We can protect witnesses, but not if you give us names we cannot verify. Tell us what you heard, not what you suspect."

Operator: Share verified evidence -> C11; share names without protection -> E10; share nothing -> C11; misroute to Home Affairs -> E11.

C11 / Kamal Das / side

The family decision

Call: From Shapla Apartments, requests Neeladesh Central Hospital. Kamal says the family has food but no safe transport and asks whether the operator's wife should leave the hospital.

If tapped: "Your wife says she will stay with the patients. She thinks the guards protect everyone. I do not think they do."

Operator: Connect hospital, workers, or Cantonment -> C12 with different family states; delay -> E12.

C12 / Colonel Arman Vey

The sealed order

Call: From Nabinagar Cantonment, requests Republic Secretariat. Arman holds an order bearing the president's seal and asks whether civilian officials issued a matching order.

If tapped: "The order says occupy the hospital and radio first. It does not say who signed it. I need to know whether the president can still speak."

Operator: Connect Secretariat and tell the truth -> C13; report truck evidence -> C13 and arrest Captain Varo; claim the president confirms it -> E13; connect Radio -> E14.

C13 / Dr. Bimal Saye

The president cannot answer

Call: From Neeladesh Central Hospital, requests National Radio Building. Bimal says the president is alive but cannot rule, confirm an order, or contradict the people speaking for him.

If tapped: "The ICU monitor is steady. His eyes are open, but he cannot form words. Anyone claiming a presidential answer is lying."

Operator: Tell Radio the truth -> C14; preserve the lie through Secretariat -> E15; call EMS -> C14 if wife safe or E16 if unsafe; choose family first -> C16.

C14 / Meera Tal

Recognition

Call: From South Neeladesh Embassy, requests Republic Secretariat. Meera asks the Secretariat to hear the council's request for recognition and a safe route for judges.

If tapped: "Recognition comes with fuel, medicine, and advisers. The council may survive, but it will owe the South more than it admits."

Operator: Connect Secretariat -> C15; misroute to Home Affairs Annex -> E17; refuse -> C15 without recognition.

C15 / Omar Fenn

The corridor

Call: From Nabinagar Central Station, requests Ratan Colony. Omar says refugees, miners, and soldiers are converging on the platform and asks which authority should receive the rail key.

If tapped: "The civilian dispatcher has the passenger list. The army officer has the fuel manifest. If I hand over the key, one of them will own the night."

Operator: Open civilian corridor -> E18 if evidence reached Suri; open army corridor -> E19; hold the line -> E20.

C16 / Kamal Das

The last call home

Call: From Shapla Apartments, requests Neeladesh Central Hospital. Kamal asks for a final decision: spend the last cash on a family vehicle, or use it to keep the political corridor open.

If tapped: "There is no political question left in this call. Tell me whether I should bring her home or wait for you to decide what the country needs."

Operator: Protect wife and child -> E21; spend the last cash on the corridor -> E22; ignore -> E23.
