NORTH NEELADESH / STORY DESIGN REVIEW

THE LINE BETWEEN
WHAT IS SAID AND WHAT HAPPENS

A four-shift branching call story for the Ministry of Communications. The operator is not a chosen hero. He is a poor switchboard worker with a wife in hospital, a newborn on the way, and access to information that powerful people cannot directly control.

Review assumptions

  • There is one audible speaker per call. No whispered second voice and no simultaneous dialogue.
  • Every caller reports, asks, or bargains in natural language. The DAG owns consequences; the LLM supplies wording and emotional variation.
  • Information is the main currency. A connected call lets one person learn from another. A tapped call creates evidence in the operator's memory, not an automatic branch.
  • Reporting a fact is an action. The recipient may arrest, protect, evacuate, censor, retaliate, or ignore the report.
  • Paid side work gives the operator food and hospital money, but can cost trust, time, evidence, or another person's safety.

State carried by the DAG

  • cash: food, transport, medicine, and hospital access.
  • wife_safe: whether the operator's wife remains safe at Neeladesh Central Hospital.
  • evidence: facts heard through calls or Tap Bridge.
  • trust: faction and family willingness to believe the operator.
  • arrests, evacuations, broadcast_control, army_control: visible political consequences.
  • death: terminal failure if the operator cannot feed the family, the wife dies, or armed retaliation reaches his home or hospital.

Implementation reading, not a story constraint

The current code was checked to understand the physical interaction: a caller is selected, the operator speaks over PTT, the Directory identifies the requested destination, the operator rings it with the crank, and a connected call can be listened to through the Tap Bridge. The story is allowed to require more. The backend will be changed to carry this DAG's cash, evidence, faction, family, and disclosure state; the existing demo records and demo plot are not being treated as canon.

Shift 1 // Trust is a routing choice

S1-A / Mina Das, Central Hospital records clerk

core call / line: Neeladesh Central Hospital

Requests connection to: Republic Secretariat

She says: The Secretariat has requested a list of people who visited the president. Mina says the list contains the operator's wife, though his wife works in a different ward. She asks him to connect her to Deputy Minister Harun so she can correct it before the list becomes an arrest order.

What this call exchanges
  • Fact: the hospital is compiling visitor names.
  • Claim: the wife may be falsely placed on the list.
  • Question: is the list for medical security or political arrests?
Player actions and DAG effects
Connect normallyMina reaches the Secretariat. If the player later verifies her identity, the wife is removed from the list. If not, the Secretariat marks Mina as a leak.
Lookup before connectingDirectory confirms Mina is hospital staff. Evidence +1; next Home Affairs call unlocks a truthful correction.
Delay or refuseWife remains on the list. The operator gains no evidence. A later hospital call becomes urgent.

S1-B / Elias Korr, lawyer at Home Affairs Annex

core call / line: Home Affairs Annex

Requests connection to: Neeladesh Central Hospital

He says: His client, Nabin Das, was taken from Shapla Apartments. He asks the hospital to confirm whether Nabin's sister is alive after a police raid. Elias says he has no proof that Nabin was arrested legally. He asks the operator to remember the time of the call.

Information chain
  • Mina can confirm a name if S1-A was connected.
  • If the player reports Elias's request to Home Affairs, Nabin is moved.
  • If the player reports it to the hospital, the sister can leave safely.
Player actions
Connect to hospitalThe family receives a medical confirmation. Trust with Elias and hospital staff rises.
Connect to Home Affairs insteadThe call is misdirected. Elias is arrested in the next shift; his evidence disappears.
Take a paid messageElias offers cash to carry the detainee's name to a judge. Cash +1, but the operator becomes a witness.

S1-S / Paro Sen, Kheyaghat Market courier

optional side call / line: Kheyaghat Market

Requests connection to: Ratan Colony

He says: A shopkeeper will pay for a warning that police are checking coal shipments. Paro asks the operator to connect him to a named storekeeper but refuses to explain which faction the warning helps. The operator needs supper money.

Choices
  • Connect Paro to Ratan Colony: cash +1; workers move supplies before a search.
  • Ask Paro to identify the storekeeper: no cash yet; learns the message is for Red Earth Workers' Congress.
  • Report the warning to police: no cash; police raid the wrong shop and Paro disappears.
  • Refuse: no political effect; cash stays low.
Survival rule

If the player refuses all paid work in Shifts 1-2 and spends time on optional verification, cash falls below the food threshold. The wife begins Shift 3 without medicine. This is a possible death path, not a moral score.

Shift 2 // Evidence must be carried somewhere

S2-A / Javed Rahman, Ratan Mining Company accountant

core call / line: Ratan Mining Company Office

Requests connection to: Nabinagar Central Station

He says: The company has stopped a coal train and blamed the workers. Javed says the shipment numbers were changed after the strike began. He asks the station dispatcher to hold the train until he can send a corrected ledger.

Information exchange
  • Javed offers a concrete ledger discrepancy.
  • The station can confirm whether the train was actually loaded.
  • Workers will act on the fact only if someone gives it to them.
Actions
Connect to Nabinagar Central StationThe train is held safely. Workers gain time; Mine Owners' Compact loses control of the public story.
Connect to Ratan ColonyWorkers hear the ledger claim but not the proof. If S1-S warned them, they protect Javed; otherwise they call him a company spy.
Report Javed to Home AffairsHe is arrested. The ledger vanishes, but the police believe the operator is useful.

S2-S / Nurse Anika Roy, Neeladesh Central Hospital

optional side call / line: Neeladesh Central Hospital

Requests connection to: Grand Neela Hotel

She says: A foreign doctor has medicine hidden at the hotel. Anika asks for the hotel pharmacist, not the government. She offers money because the hospital supply room is being watched. She asks whether the operator can keep his wife's ward out of the conversation.

Actions
  • Connect to Grand Neela Hotel: medicine arrives; cash +1; the wife is safer.
  • Connect to Republic Secretariat: medicine is seized for the president; wife's ward loses supplies.
  • Ask for the pharmacist's name first: learns South Neeladesh is involved; no immediate medicine.
  • Refuse: cash stays low; a hospital shortage increases later.
Single-speaker TTS note

Anika reports every other person's words herself. The hotel pharmacist is never an audible second speaker in this call.

S2-T / Tap evidence: Nabinagar Central Station to Ratan Mining Company Office

tap opportunity / one speaker: Mira Halek

Connection requested by: Mira Halek, railway dispatcher. Destination: Ratan Mining Company Office

Tap content: Mira reports that a military truck, not a worker train, was loaded at the mine. She names Captain Varo and a departure time. The operator hears this alone through the Tap Bridge.

Tap rule

Tapping only creates evidence: military truck at mine. It does not change the DAG. The player must later say the fact to someone.

Later disclosure branches
  • Tell Home Affairs: Captain Varo is arrested; army trust -1.
  • Tell Ratan Colony: workers block the military shipment; army pressure rises.
  • Tell Nabinagar Cantonment: the army destroys the paper trail but protects the operator from police.
  • Tell nobody: no immediate effect; the fact can still matter in Shift 4 if remembered.

Shift 3 // A fact can save one person and endanger another

S3-A / Dilan Bose, National Radio technician

core call / line: National Radio Building

Requests connection to: Nabinagar Cantonment

He says: The transmitter room has been locked by a state censor. Dilan asks the army to send guards, but says a public announcement is due in twenty minutes. He cannot tell whether the army will protect the station or seize it.

Actions
  • Connect army: transmitter is secured; army_control +1. A truthful broadcast remains possible.
  • Connect Republic Secretariat: ruling party controls the announcement; broadcast_control +1 for the party.
  • Connect Home Affairs: police raid the technicians; Dilan disappears.
  • Delay while using Tap Bridge: hears Dilan report that the false announcement will blame Riverland Autonomy League.
Information consequence

If the player later tells Laleh Mir the planned accusation, the autonomy evacuation begins early. If he tells police, the police arrest the named organizers instead.

S3-B / Laleh Mir, Riverland Autonomy League organizer

core call / line: Shapla Apartments

Requests connection to: Ratan Colony

She says: Minority families in Shapla Apartments need vehicles. Laleh asks Ratan Colony for drivers and fuel. She says the operator should not repeat the names of children, because police listen to the line. She wants only a yes or no from the colony.

Actions
  • Connect to Ratan Colony: evacuation capacity rises; workers and autonomy organizers cooperate.
  • Connect to Nabinagar Cantonment: army escort may protect the families, or confine them as suspects.
  • Connect to Home Affairs: police receive names and arrest autonomy organizers.
  • Refuse: evacuation is delayed. If the radio accusation was broadcast, wife_safe and several families can be lost.
Told facts

Laleh can act on the radio evidence only if the operator reports it in plain language. The DAG does not infer that he shared what he heard.

S3-S / Tomas Vale, foreign journalist at Grand Neela Hotel

optional side call / line: Grand Neela Hotel

Requests connection to: South Neeladesh Embassy

He says: He will pay for the room number of a People's Transition Council lawyer. He wants to publish proof that South Neeladesh is financing the council. The lawyer may be seeking asylum, not selling the country.

Actions
  • Give the room number: cash +2; the lawyer is exposed and the council fractures.
  • Connect Tomas to South Neeladesh Embassy without the room number: no cash; the embassy learns a journalist is investigating.
  • Protect the room number: trust +1 with the council; cash remains low.
  • Report Tomas to Home Affairs: journalist detained; foreign pressure rises.
Family consequence

Cash can prevent the hospital from discharging the operator's wife. A political victory with no cash can still become a family loss.

Shift 4 // The operator decides what counts as truth

S4-A / Colonel Arman Vey, National Army Command

core call / line: Nabinagar Cantonment

Requests connection to: Republic Secretariat

He says: He holds an order bearing the president's seal. It commands the army to occupy the radio, station, hospital, and Ministry. Arman asks whether the Secretariat has issued a matching order and whether the president can still speak. He asks the operator to choose a destination that proves who controls the state.

Actions
  • Connect Secretariat: if broadcast_control is low, civilian officials contradict the order; if high, the occupation is legitimized.
  • Connect National Radio Building: army announces emergency rule; army_control +2.
  • Connect Neeladesh Central Hospital: the president's doctors deny the order; the hospital becomes a military target.
  • Report the military-truck evidence: Arman arrests Captain Varo and may trust the operator.
Failure condition

If the operator falsely claims the president can speak, Arman sends soldiers to the hospital. If wife_safe=false, the personal ending triggers before the political ending.

S4-B / Advocate Suri Nahal, People's Transition Council

core call / line: Republic Secretariat

Requests connection to: South Neeladesh Embassy

She says: The council needs recognition and a safe route for three judges. Suri asks whether the operator has proof of arrests, the radio accusation, or the military shipment. She offers no money. She offers legal protection if the transition succeeds.

Actions
  • Share verified evidence: civilian transition gains legitimacy; South Neeladesh influence rises.
  • Share only the radio evidence: autonomy protection improves but the council cannot prove the army's plan.
  • Share nothing: council negotiates blind; army or ruling party may take the capital.
  • Connect Home Affairs instead: Suri is arrested and the civilian ending is closed.
Truth condition

Evidence that was never heard or never reported cannot be selected. A caller may ask for a fact, but the player must have obtained it through an earlier connection or tap.

S4-S / Kamal Das, the operator's father-in-law at Shapla Apartments

optional side call / line: Shapla Apartments

Requests connection to: Neeladesh Central Hospital

He says: The family has food but no safe transport. He asks whether the operator's wife should leave the hospital now or stay behind its guards. He wants a direct answer, not politics.

Actions
  • Connect to hospital and request discharge: wife leaves if an evacuation route exists.
  • Connect to Ratan Colony: workers provide a vehicle if their trust is high.
  • Connect to Nabinagar Cantonment: military transport arrives, but the family is recorded as a protected political household.
  • Ignore or delay: if the hospital is occupied, wife_safe becomes false.
Human ending gate

This call is not a political puzzle. It checks whether the player protected the person they repeatedly risked everything for.
