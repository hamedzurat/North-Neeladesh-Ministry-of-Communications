# World

North Neeladesh was created three years ago when a bloody war over ethnic boundaries divided the larger country of Neeladesh.

The border is new and the graves are new. Government offices are full of people who were soldiers, collaborators, refugees, or grieving relatives only three years ago. Nobody fully agrees that the war is over.

The president has ruled North Neeladesh since the division. His government is responsible for killings and mob violence against minorities.

An unknown attacker has now shot the president. Nobody knows who ordered the assassination attempt. The president remains alive in intensive care, but he cannot rule, resign, confirm an order, or contradict anyone claiming to speak for him.

North Neeladesh has become a bedside state. The ruling party controls access to the president, conceals the severity of his condition, and continues issuing orders in his name. The army, secret police, political opposition, armed nationalists, minority autonomy movement, mine owners, and South Neeladesh are all trying to shape what replaces him.

The factions fight each other for power. They do not revolve around the operator or his family.
