NORTH NEELADESH / STORY DESIGN REVIEW

EVERY CALL OPENS A DIFFERENT ROAD

A node is one call. Each outcome is an edge to another call, a state change, or an ending. The LLM supplies natural language for the named caller; the DAG decides what the caller knows, what the operator can report, and what happens next.

Player state: money, rating, five information flags, and per-character progression flags. Money and rating are the only global variables. A Tap Bridge stores information; it changes the DAG only when the operator discloses it to somebody who can act on it. One call has one audible speaker.

World settings

  • North Neeladesh was created three years ago when an ethnic war split Neeladesh. The border and graves are both recent.
  • The president has ruled since partition. His government is responsible for killings and mob violence against minorities.
  • An unknown attacker shot the president. He is alive in intensive care but cannot rule, confirm an order, or contradict anyone speaking for him.
  • The country is a bedside state: the ruling party controls access to the ICU while the army, secret police, opposition, nationalists, autonomy movement, and South Neeladesh compete for succession.
  • The operator's father became popular during the war and was killed by sympathizers after it ended. The ministry gave the operator a job to support the bereaved family.
  • A neighboring member of the persecuted minority was killed by a mob. The operator remembers the neighbor by name, but is not himself a member of that minority.
  • The operator is twenty-four, works at the Ministry of Communications, and has a pregnant wife at the hospital.
  • He dislikes the nepotism that put him behind the switchboard, but cannot afford to refuse the job.
  • The operator is not a faction member. At first the factions do not know his name and treat him as infrastructure. His power comes from deciding who can speak to whom.
  • The switchboard has twelve subscriber lines, one Tap Bridge, emergency Police/EMS calls, and a Directory whose records describe what the state believes, not necessarily what is true.
  • Side characters and their relationships with the operator matter even when they are not deciding national power.

Time and tone

  • The game covers four work shifts. Fourteen main calls occupy fixed slots, with side calls mixed into the route. M11 and M14 each have authored variants selected by state.
  • Every call has one audible named speaker. Other people's words are reported or quoted by that speaker.
  • The LLM supplies natural wording and personality. The DAG supplies facts, permitted knowledge, destinations, actions, and outcomes.
  • The story is not a mystery about the operator's father. It is a pressure story about information, survival, and institutions deciding who may speak.
  • Money is practical: food, medicine, transport, and hospital access. It is not a morality score.

Possible game edges

Every main call supports connection, expiry, and refusal. Police, EMS, information disclosure, and paid relays appear only where the call authors a consequence. Looking up a record, tapping, holding, talking, or accepting money modify an outcome; they are not separate edge types.

EdgeWhen it firesWhat it can change
Successful connectionThe operator connects the caller to the requested place before the call expires.Money +1 for completed work. Rating changes only when the connection was clearly competent or harmful; the call advances its authored route.
Call expiredThe timer runs out while the caller waits, talks, or remains on hold.Deducts money for a missed call, usually lowers rating, and advances the world without the requested conversation taking place.
Connection refusedThe operator explicitly denies the caller's request.Records an intentional refusal. Its rating effect depends on what the operator could reasonably know; the world advances without the requested conversation.
Reported to policeThe operator sends a caller's identity, location, or claim to Police.Only authored reports produce this edge. Police may protect, question, raid, arrest, or ignore; an innocent or lawful report lowers rating.
Saved by EMSThe operator gives EMS enough correct information to reach a medical emergency in time.Saves a person and raises rating. An incomplete, false, or late report does not produce this edge.
Information disclosedThe operator repeats a known fact to a caller, destination, Police, or EMS.Resolves by fact, recipient, timing, and accuracy. Tapping alone stores the fact and does not disclose it.

Global variables

VariableGoes upGoes down
MoneyCompleting calls, taking a bribe, receiving a gift, or accepting Asha Sen's inheritance.Missing calls, paying for medicine or transport, buying something, or losing wages through bad work.

Information flags

Facts are learned by questions, Directory checks, or Tap Bridge listening. Tapping stores a fact; it becomes consequential only when disclosed to somebody able to act.

PRESIDENT_SILENT The president is conscious but cannot speak, dictate, confirm, or contradict an order.

ORDER_CONFLICT Orders 17-A and 17-B share a seal and serial number but give incompatible instructions.

LIST_SOURCE Schedule R-12 contains dead man Mahir Tal at Shapla, Block C, Flat 4B.

BRIGADE_LEAK The Brigade has R-12 addresses and receives Police patrol gaps from Home Affairs.

ARMY_MOVEMENT Arman sends one column toward Radio and another toward Central Station.

Threshold endings

Check both variables after every resolved call. Crossing a threshold interrupts the current route immediately. Exact values should be set during balancing.

TriggerEnding
Money at or below the low thresholdBankruptcy. The operator runs out of money and cannot keep his household afloat.
Money at or above the high thresholdA Better Country. The operator has enough money to leave North Neeladesh with his family and start again somewhere safer.
Rating at or below the low thresholdLet Go. The ministry fires the operator because he is bad at the job.
Rating at or above the high thresholdInsubordination. The operator performs so well that senior officials notice he is making judgments instead of blindly following their orders. They fire him for "insubordination."

Main story draft: The political route below mirrors story/main-story-draft.md for review. It contains fourteen scheduled main calls, eighteen authored main-call nodes, five information flags, physical station selection, and layered political and human outcomes.

Default route and station selection

If the operator asks no useful questions, performs no taps, discloses nothing, never calls Police, and completes every requested connection, Radio carries the party bulletin, Home Affairs confirms addresses, the Brigade attacks, and the Army mobilizes under 17-B.

Default political ending: Temporary Command. The Army takes Radio and Central Station while arrests and Brigade violence continue elsewhere.

  1. Use M14R when PLATFORM_SIX_OPEN: M9 connected and the exact relay reached verified Laleh in M11A.
  2. Otherwise use M14A when ARMY_AT_STATION: M6 connected or expired, or M12 dispatched a delayed column, unless M12 redirected it to Shapla.
  3. Otherwise use M14P when HOME_AFFAIRS_TRANSFER_READY: M11B connected successfully.
  4. Otherwise use M14C.

WIFE_PROTECTED remains true when Laleh was reported and M10 connected. If conditional after M10, reporting Laleh in M11A restores it. M10 expiry or refusal makes it false.

Core conflict

By the first shift, two versions of Emergency Order 17 circulate with the same presidential seal and serial number. 17-A gives Home Affairs authority to detain Schedule R-12, leaves the railway civilian, and orders the Army to protect government buildings. 17-B gives the Army emergency command of Radio and Central Station and orders R-12 detainees transferred to the Cantonment.

Nobody proves who wrote either copy. The party, Army, Directorate, and South Neeladesh each use the contradiction to advance their own power. The author is never revealed.

Operator position

Callers see a night operator who can connect, refuse, delay, listen, check the Directory, call Police or EMS, and repeat something heard earlier. They do not know the full pattern of his choices.

  • Home Affairs orders operators to report IDs marked DETAIN AND REPORT.
  • Javed offers money to refuse Varo; Paro offers money to relay an exact phrase to Laleh.
  • A connection audit identifies the operator and can expose his wife through ministry dependent records.
  • South offers the registered household an escape route for accurate information about the Brigade leak and Army movement.

Radio, Station, Home Affairs, and armed groups follow different orders. Arrests, attacks, and fighting continue without one authority.
