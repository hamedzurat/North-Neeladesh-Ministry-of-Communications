# Prototype: three-minute university trailer

This is a throwaway storyboard for review. It answers [Storyboard the three-minute university trailer](https://github.com/hamedzurat/North-Neeladesh-Ministry-of-Communications/issues/26).

Use Cabinet footage throughout, except for the setting map, captions, and title cards. A calm human narrator reads the Ministry copy. Relay clicks, ringing, paper feed, and light percussion form the score.

## Twenty-second handoff

Hold the final frame while the demonstrator moves to the Cabinet and says:

> This is the Cabinet. Relief Train 7 has reached Kharad. The shift starts now.

At twenty seconds, switch automatically to the live overhead camera and start the Run. Taren's real Line Lamp lights on the cut.

## Capture rule

The local-AI exchange must be one complete captured turn from the target laptop. Silence may be shortened, but words may not be stitched together. Caption key narration and all Subscriber speech.
